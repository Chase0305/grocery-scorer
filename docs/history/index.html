  1. SEP 9 · MORNING

    Five peanut butters

    It started with one aisle: collect every peanut butter ShopRite sells, rank them by hand, then build a scoring engine and see if it agreed. A progress screen watched the collector work; the first results page ranked 45 jars.

    45 productsfirst scoring engineOpen Food Facts link-up
  2. SEP 9 · AFTERNOON

    Four categories and a blind review

    Yogurt, bread and ground beef joined. A review page listed products alphabetically, with no scores, so your own ranking stayed unbiased, and the engine was tuned until it matched.

    yogurt additiveswhole-grain scoringmissing data no longer counts as perfect
  3. SEP 14

    The whole store, and three designs

    The crawler went store-wide (17,787 products), meat got sourcing and carcinogen warnings, and three looks were mocked up: a dark console, a friendly market and a newspaper-style ledger. The market won and became the app.

    17,787 productsmeat sourcingcarcinogen citations
  4. SEP 18

    Every product scored, and it works on a phone

    Scores went from about 1,700 products to over 15,000: filters, warning banners, a price signal, sweetener caps and a phone layout.

    15,764 scoredfiltersphone layout
  5. SEP 29

    From scores to shopping

    A shopping list grouped by aisle, diet profiles, editable ingredient severities, and the big idea: start from a meal and get every ingredient's healthiest pick. "Buy it or make it" compared store versions with homemade.

    shopping listdiet profilesmeal cards with photosbuy or make
  6. SEP 30

    Dinner picker, 73 meals, recipe import

    The busiest build day yet: a "What's for dinner?" flowchart, 73 meals with photos, a 2-3x faster load, a weekly planner, recipe import from links or pasted text, and cheaper mode.

    73 meals97% less data to loadweek plannerrecipe import
  7. OCT 2

    New look, sharper scores, helpers that work overnight

    A redesign inspired by Trader Joe's, Whole Foods and EWG; a scoring deep dive that put Coke at 15 and Poppi well above it; a label scanner; recipes for every meal; and the first agents: a night fetcher and a daily researcher.

    design v2scoring fixes A+B+Clabel scannernight fetch agentresearch agent
  8. OCT 3 · TODAY

    Polish, real recipes, agents in the cloud

    Research moved to the cloud on Opus and a weekly fishy-scores check was added. A processing-rating fix moved 2,154 scores. Twelve wrong meal photos were replaced, all 82 recipes now follow tested sources, the dinner picker asks about cravings and spice, and hosting is ready to switch on.

    cloud agentsNOVA fix82 sourced recipesnew dinner questionshosting ready
